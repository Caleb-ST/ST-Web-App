General warranty rules
A warranty covers faults caused by manufacturing defects only. It does not cover normal wear and tear, consumable parts such as drill chucks, brushes, blades or belts, accidental damage, misuse, modifications, improper maintenance, physical damage, tampering or unauthorised repair. Proof of purchase is required. All M & H class vacuums must be submitted directly through the supplier by the customer due to hazardous material concerns.
