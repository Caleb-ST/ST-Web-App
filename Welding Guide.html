What am I looking at?

Contact tip: the small metal part the wire passes through.
Gas nozzle / shroud: the outer sleeve directing shielding gas.
Tip holder / diffuser: the part behind the tip; it also needs to match.
Torch: the gun and lead. Its front end determines the consumables.
